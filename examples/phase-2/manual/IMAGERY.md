# 静读 / Margin — Imagery

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Medium and Purpose

J-08 explicitly rejects decorative photography for this text-led reading scope. Article titles and synopses provide both subject and reflective mood, so no missing image prevents the product’s core decision.

## Subject Camera Crop and Ratios

Subject, camera and crop are not applicable because no production image inventory is required. The style tile visibly demonstrates this deliberate absence instead of generating filler art.

## Lighting Depth Background Grade and Texture

Paper-like warmth is expressed through opaque canvas color, not a noisy texture. Lighting, depth and grading are inapplicable; readable type has priority over simulated material.

## Mask and Density

Image density is zero by product decision. No image masks are defined. A future illustrated article would need a content case and a revised imagery strategy before adding ratios.

## Fallback Placeholder and Loading

Fallback is the complete title and synopsis, which already form the main layout. There is no shimmer image placeholder. Loading tells the reader an article is opening without inventing a media box.

## Generation and Asset Direction

No AI image generation is needed in this example. If the scope later adds editorial illustration, define subject, crop, exclusions and asset rights separately; never generate the UI grid as an image.
