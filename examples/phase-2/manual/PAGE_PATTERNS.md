# 静读 / Margin — Page Patterns

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Scope and Pattern Inventory

Hero Decision (compact and wide), Editorial List and Detail Narrative cover the current choose/archive/read tasks. This is a content-derived inventory, not a mandatory set for other products.

## Pattern Definitions

Hero Decision: choose one essay; best for today’s feature; title is focal; title → synopsis → action; low then medium density; title owns the larger region; normal scroll; read/save/summary permitted; avoid equal card grids; stack on compact. Editorial List: scan past essays; heading then linear title rows; medium density; rows separated by space; search/navigation permitted; avoid cards; preserve list under reflow. Detail Narrative: read; title then uninterrupted body; body owns page length; progress/navigation permitted; avoid floating overlays; bound measure on wide and reflow on compact.

## Responsive Variants and Content Stress

Atlas pattern IDs essay-compact, essay-wide, archive and reading map to these definitions. Long title stress grows the compact title region and pushes following content down. Do not shrink every region to preserve a decorative silhouette.
