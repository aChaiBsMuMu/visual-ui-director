# 静读 / Margin — Do Dont

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Project-specific Paired Rules

DO: one leading essay title and one committed accent (DNA-1/3, J-01/02). DON’T: six equally loud cards. DO: whitespace between article groups (DNA-2, J-05). DON’T: card inside card. DO: selected filter has a small field. DON’T: give every icon a circle.

## Failure Signals and Visual Evidence

In component-specimen.svg compare reading and saving emphasis; in layout-atlas.svg compare title and synopsis areas; in typography-specimen.svg compare heading character and body leading. Failure means secondary elements attract the first glance, not merely that a token differs.
