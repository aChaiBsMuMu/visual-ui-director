# 静读 / Margin — Motion

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Purpose and Character

J-09: motion communicates save feedback and reading continuity. Character is short and interruptible; content remains still. Decorative breathing, floating or auto-advancing article motion is prohibited.

## Triggers and State Changes

Press confirms the reading action; selection updates the language filter; loading labels retain space; success confirms saved position; error preserves the article and exposes retry. Navigation restores reading position. Expansion and data charts are outside the inventory.

## Timing Easing and Evidence Labels

Feedback and transition token values are explicitly Proposed, not Observed in the static reference. Easing follows the same evidence label. The SVG motion cue communicates relationships only, not runtime timing.

## Interruption and Reduced Motion

Reduced motion immediately switches state labels and maintains position; interruption cancels stale navigation feedback. Loading cannot keep animating after the article opens or after a failure.

## Prohibited Motion and Scope Exceptions

No ornamental loops, expressive hero animation or large background transition. Expansion motion is excluded because no expanding region exists; state communication remains required despite that bounded exception.
