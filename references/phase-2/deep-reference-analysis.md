# Deep reference analysis

Analysis is a causal argument, not a feature inventory. For each important finding use **WHAT → WHY → EFFECT → FIT → ADAPT → RULE**, optionally RISK and ANTI-PATTERN. Assign a stable finding ID, source reference and evidence location. WHY is normally Inferred even when WHAT is Observed. A new RULE is normally Proposed. A finding may conclude Reject, not just Adopt.

Observed means directly visible or reliably measured. Inferred means a plausible explanation, with uncertainty. Proposed means our adaptation. Exact font names, dimensions, breakpoints, interaction behavior and motion parameters require reliable supporting evidence to be Observed; a static screenshot alone does not establish them. Record measurement method and viewport when measuring. Approximate area ratios must remain estimates.

Each dimension needs at least one complete chain, or evidence-backed explanation that it is out of scope. Keep components and icons separate. Reference source IDs must agree with the contract and influence map.

## Questions that expose mechanisms

What makes the interface look deliberately designed? What dominates the first, second and third glance? How does area establish hierarchy? Where is space generous, where intentionally dense? Why do colors appear there, and how often? Why is one group contained in a card and another not? Why break the grid? How do heading and body contrast through width, scale, weight or spacing? Do icons lead, explain or support text? Does geometry vary by role? Are images informational or emotional, and does the page survive their absence? Which gesture creates recognition? Which changes destroy identity? Separate decoration from mechanisms that organize content or decisions.
