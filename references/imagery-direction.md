# Imagery direction

Choose the media system that carries the product's emotional and informational load: photography, illustration, 3D, product render, iconography, texture, or background treatment.

For each permitted medium define:

- subject and narrative role
- camera/view angle and subject scale
- crop, focal position, and aspect-ratio family
- lighting, saturation, contrast, and grading
- background, texture, people, and environment
- mask/container behavior and responsive crop
- loading, missing-asset, and low-resolution fallbacks
- licensing, generation, and attribution constraints

Name prohibited mixtures. Do not alternate casually between lifestyle photography, flat illustration, glossy 3D, and dreamlike generated imagery. If multiple media are required, define where each belongs and the grading or geometry that makes them one family. Review contact sheets across screens to detect drift.
