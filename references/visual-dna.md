# Visual DNA

Use after Gate A and before tokens. Visual DNA is the 5–8-rule identity layer used by design, implementation, and screenshot review.

## Derivation

1. Start from the Reference Contract, product intent, content, and platform context.
2. Convert reference traits into observable behavior: what dominates, what recedes, how space separates, how imagery behaves, and what creates recognition.
3. Add a memorable visual gesture for every key screen. A gesture must affect composition, content reveal, image proportion, information shape, or meaningful motion; radius, shadow, gradient, and colorful icons alone do not qualify.
4. Test each rule against a screenshot: two reviewers should be able to judge match, partial, or violation.

## Output contract

Create VISUAL_DNA.md with:

- 5–8 numbered principles
- evidence source: Observed, Inferred, or Proposed
- visible pass condition
- named anti-pattern or failure signal
- key-screen visual gestures
- version and approval status

Keep token values elsewhere. Replace vague adjectives such as “clean”, “modern”, “premium”, or “beautiful” with visual evidence. Example: “Section boundaries rely on 48–72 px whitespace and type-scale changes; neutral card borders are reserved for interactive containment.”

## Coherence check

- The principles distinguish this product without its logo.
- They do not conflict with selected or rejected traits.
- They survive platform adaptation.
- They are specific enough to use in Screenshot Critic and drift audits.
