# 静读 / Margin — Reference Analysis

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system. Source R1 is [the original synthetic editorial study](evidence/reference-study.svg).

## 01 Creative Thesis

F-01 / R1 — fictional, locally authored reference, not a claim about an external product.

WHAT — Observed (synthetic R1 study): one large editorial title leads the synthetic R1 page.
WHY — Inferred: avoid asking the reader to compare many choices.
EFFECT — Inferred: a single first-glance focal object.
FIT — a reader choosing one essay benefits from lower decision pressure.
ADAPT — Proposed: use our own Chinese essay title rather than the study’s identity.
RULE — J-01: one essay title leads each decision viewport.
RISK / ANTI-PATTERN — secondary decoration or rigid copied geometry defeats this product’s reading task; inspect the named specimen and compact screen.
