# 静读 / Margin — Iconography

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Production Source and Character

Custom original fixture paths use one outline language. They are not claimed to be Lucide, SF Symbols or another installed library. Production would use this controlled custom family or explicitly replace it under a new source decision.

## Grid Stroke Cap Join and Corner

Grid, stroke, round caps and joins come from icon tokens. Bookmark, back and search share weight and corner behavior. No mixed fill styles enter from the reference.

## Optical Sizes

Small supports metadata, regular supports labels and large supports standalone labeled controls. Sizes are token references; optical consistency must be judged at all shown sizes rather than assuming geometric scaling is sufficient.

## States and Containers

Default is muted outline, active is text ink, selected is brand outline on a small rectangular field only when selection needs containment. No universal circular background is allowed.

## Color and Accessible Labels

Icon colors follow text and state roles; every standalone control has an accessible label such as 返回文章列表. Decorative duplicates of adjacent text are hidden from assistive technology.

## Icon and Label Relationship

Icons sit alongside short task labels with micro spacing. The text names the action; the icon assists recognition. Neither may compete with the essay’s dominant title.
