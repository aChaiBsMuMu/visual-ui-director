## Tradeoffs and Rejected Alternatives

Editorial expression wins for title and whitespace; browser-native behavior wins for navigation, search and focus. Reject equal previews, copied identity and decorative gradients. A future comparison-heavy product would reverse J-01; this one chooses a single essay.

## Rules and Failure Signals

J-01/DNA-1 fails when equal-weight previews lead the first glance. J-02/DNA-3 fails when save and read have equal color area. J-05/DNA-2 fails when ordinary prose gains repeated containment. J-09/DNA-5 fails when feedback displaces or covers the reading position.
