# 静读 / Margin — Layout

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Grid and Alignment Anchors

Compact uses one aligned content column; wide gives the essay title the dominant left region and synopsis/action a narrower right region. Margins and space roles derive from actual content rather than a universal grid formula.

## Visual Hierarchy and Reading Path

First title, second synopsis, third committed action. Publication metadata and navigation remain tertiary. The primary title region must dominate both spatially and typographically.

## Density and Whitespace Rhythm

Low density at the decision title, medium density in archive and body paragraphs. Micro spacing binds labels; component spacing groups controls; section and macro roles separate editorial thoughts.

## Dominance Ratio

Wide title receives the larger region; compact title wraps into the leading block. The atlas expresses approximate relationships, not measured reference dimensions. A secondary save action cannot acquire an equal color footprint.

## Controlled Rule Breaking

Only the leading title may exceed ordinary content measure on a wide decision page. Search, reading body and archive rows remain aligned. Grid breaks require J-04 fit and must not cause horizontal scrolling.

## Fixed and Scrolling Behavior

Normal document scrolling preserves browser expectations. No fixed-height hero traps long text. Reading feedback stays adjacent to its trigger and never overlays the current paragraph.

## Responsive Transformation

Wide-to-compact stacks the title, synopsis and action; archive remains a linear list and detail retains the reading column. At the smallest target, long titles are reflowed before changing hierarchy.
