# Motion direction

Motion communicates state, causality, spatial relationship, hierarchy, and brand character. “Use subtle animation” is not a specification.

Define purpose and personality, entry, exit, feedback, transition, loading, duration/easing families, interruption, reduced-motion equivalents, and prohibited motion.

Map every motion to a trigger, property, duration/easing token, interruption rule, and reduced-motion outcome. Avoid constant floating, gratuitous zoom, spring-heavy playfulness, or decorative motion without state meaning unless the approved Visual DNA explicitly requires it.

Static references can suggest motion character but cannot prove implementation. Label extracted motion as Inferred or Proposed unless video or interactive evidence exists.
