# Typography character

Define how typography behaves, not only its sizes.

For Heading, Body, Metric, Label, and any editorial or data-specialized role, specify:

- character in observable terms
- classification, width, x-height, weight contrast, case, and tracking
- type hierarchy and alignment behavior
- line height and line-length range
- numeric style and tabular/proportional behavior
- multilingual and fallback strategy
- fluid scaling or native text-style mapping
- prohibited habits such as uppercase-heavy labels or uniform weights

Unknown reference fonts must remain Inferred. Propose licensed, available, or system-safe equivalents and test them with real Chinese and Latin content. Screenshot QA should catch weak role contrast, awkward wrapping, clipped Dynamic Type, inconsistent numerals, and line measures that damage composition.
