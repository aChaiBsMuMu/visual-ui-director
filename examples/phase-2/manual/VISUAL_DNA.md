# 静读 / Margin — Visual Dna

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Principles

1. One essay title dominates the decision viewport; neighboring choices remain visually subordinate.
2. Whitespace separates editorial groups before a border or card is considered.
3. The commitment action concentrates brand emphasis; save and navigation retain lower visual weight.
4. Song-style titles and neutral body/UI text establish a visible role contrast without arbitrary boldness.
5. Reading progress and feedback preserve the position and continuity of the text column.
6. A consistent outline icon language supports words and never replaces the primary essay focus.

## Key-screen Visual Gestures

Home: dominant Chinese title with open space, failing when previews become equal. Archive: rhythmic uncontained title rows, failing when every article gains a card. Reading: uninterrupted text column, failing when sticky feedback overlays text.

## Approval

The numbered behaviors become approved only with the version’s Gate B lock. REVIEW.json contains individual source, pass and failure tests for screenshot critique.
