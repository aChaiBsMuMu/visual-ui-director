# Anti-generic and anti-AI UI

Use during hypothesis review, style-tile review, and Screenshot Critic. A pattern is a risk signal, not an automatic ban; require a product, content, platform, or brand rationale.

## Ten common patterns

1. **Dashboard formula:** title, subtitle, three stats, four feature cards, CTA without task logic.
2. **Card everything:** white surfaces, 16 px radius, gray border, and soft shadow used for every content group.
3. **Default AI gradient:** blue-purple glow, floating orb, neon highlight, or glass decoration without brand evidence.
4. **Icon-title-paragraph repetition:** every module uses the same feature-grid anatomy.
5. **Mechanical spacing:** one interval repeated across micro, component, section, macro, and hero relationships.
6. **Excessive pills:** controls, tags, navigation, filters, and containers all use capsule geometry.
7. **Generic hero:** oversized centered heading, paragraph, CTA, and decorative blob regardless of content.
8. **Unnecessary glassmorphism:** blur and transparency without hierarchy, platform, or brand purpose.
9. **Design system dominates product:** the page reads as a component showcase rather than the user's content and task.
10. **No memorable gesture:** the key screen has no intentional, product-specific visual moment.

## Critique response

Name the observed pattern, visible evidence, why it harms this product, generic-risk level (Low, Medium, High), and a structural correction. Prefer removing unnecessary containers, changing hierarchy, varying rhythm, strengthening content-specific composition, or creating a meaningful gesture before adding decoration.
