# 静读 / Margin — Master

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Creative Thesis

One essay earns attention through a dominant title and a quiet, continuous reading surface. See DESIGN_STRATEGY.md for the causal argument.

## Product Character

Editorial reading content with calm, explicit controls: Song-style headings, warm neutral canvas, a concentrated brick reading action and uncontained article rows.

## Design Priorities

Title comprehension → reading continuity → discovery. Visual novelty cannot displace a readable title or browser-native navigation.

## Design Tensions

Editorial for content, systematic for controls; spacious decision region, denser archive. Navigation behavior remains native while typography expresses identity.

## Visual DNA Summary

DNA-1 one essay focus; DNA-2 space before containment; DNA-3 concentrated commitment accent; DNA-4 title/body character contrast; DNA-5 stable reading continuity; DNA-6 icons support text. Full tests live in VISUAL_DNA.md and REVIEW.json.

## Color Summary

Warm canvas with dark ink, brick only for commitment and selection; state colors retain independent meaning. COLOR.md describes usage; TOKENS.json owns values.

## Typography Summary

Song-style display/title contrast with neutral interface labels and spacious body leading. TYPOGRAPHY.md specifies multilingual behavior and fallback review.

## Layout Grammar

Hero Decision for one essay, Editorial List for archive, Detail Narrative for reading. Compact stacks title and synopsis; wide lets title occupy the larger region.

## Geometry Summary

Square editorial surfaces, modest control corners and smaller selected-filter corners. Whitespace groups prose; borders indicate editing. See SPACING_GEOMETRY.md.

## Component Character

One high-emphasis reading action, a receding save action, uncontained essay summary, bounded search and concise state feedback. The inventory follows actual reading tasks.

## Icon Character

One original outline family, common stroke and grid, no default icon circles. Icons retain adjacent text or an accessible label.

## Imagery Character

No decorative photography: title and synopsis carry information and mood. The system remains legible without any image asset.

## Motion Character

Short proposed feedback and continuity transitions; immediate reduced-motion state change. No looping or gratuitous editorial animation.

## Signature Gestures

The dominant serif Chinese essay title against open neutral space is the identity gesture. It survives compact layout through wrapping, not miniature scaling.

## Platform Rules

Browser-native links, semantic controls, visible focus and normal document scrolling. Compact widths stack content; watch adaptations are outside this web-only example.
