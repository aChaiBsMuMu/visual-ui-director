# 静读 / Margin — Spacing Geometry

Fictional demonstration; proposed design, not a sampled production system.

## Spacing Roles

Micro joins icon and label; component pads controls; group connects summary metadata; section separates article groups; macro and hero give the title breathing room. See space tokens and the actual length comparison sheet.

## Radius Roles

Control corners signal operability; selected filters are smaller rectangular fields; article surfaces and hero remain square. These role differences follow J-05 and avoid a pill vocabulary unrelated to reading.

## Stroke Border and Divider

Inputs use a visible boundary and focus uses its own ring. Quiet dividers can group archive entries; prose paragraphs have no border. A no-border token explicitly expresses uncontained content.

## Surface Relationships

Warm canvas supports continuous text; surface supports an editable or feedback region; elevated is reserved for temporary UI. Do not wrap the article in repeated surfaces merely to show a token.

## Elevation Shadow Blur and Translucency

No shadow on permanent editorial content. Only transient feedback may use the overlay elevation token. Blur and translucency are excluded because text continuity and contrast take priority over material spectacle.

## Container Rules and Image Mask

Containment requires editing or transient feedback. No image mask is needed because imagery is excluded; future illustrations would require a new judged direction and version rather than an ad hoc rounded card.
